# CYMA-1 demo 06 (spec §8.4): FROZEN GONG drone. CATHEDRAL GONG (bronze, BELL+ stiffness, negative BLOOM: an opera-gong

# notes ring and decay over them. SELECT nudges the shape: frozen partials glide too. About 30 s.
