# CYMA-1 noise floor (spec §3.10.6-7, §3.11, §8.2). MASTER is a float gain after the limiter, quantised once to
# 24 bits with no dither, so:
#   noise_silence.wav  at master 1448 (the spec's "-20 dB position": (1448/4096)^2 is -18 dB), nothing playing:
#                      exact zeros
#   noise_tone.wav     a sustained bowed tone at the same MASTER: every low bit alive. tools/golden.py looks at the
#                      bottom byte of each sample between the marks: >= 250 of its 256 values seen, and no more
#                      than 2 % of samples with it zero (a 16-bit path would leave it zero every time)
#   instrument/build/host/cyma-1_host instrument/tests/noise_floor.txt OUTDIR
wait 1700
tour 0
master 1448
wait 300
peakreset
wav24 out:noise_silence.wav
wait 3000
wav24stop
expect peakq 0
#
# ---- a sustained tone: preset 09 BOWED GLASS PAD, C4 leaned on
preset 9
wait 400
peakreset
wav24 out:noise_tone.wav
wait 200
key 7 down 100
wait 300
press_all 80
wait 1200
#@mark tone_on
wait 4000
#@mark tone_off
key 7 up
wait 1500
wav24stop
expect peakq >0
expect nan 0
# and back to silence: the tail ends in exact zeros again (the room is 6 s here)
wait 40000
peakreset
wait 2000
expect peakq 0
metrics
