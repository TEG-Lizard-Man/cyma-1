# CYMA-1 boot splash (spec §6.7): its length, and the cut. Left alone, the credit holds until 4.5 s (v1.1.2: 3 s longer,
# long enough to read), the logo and its tagline hold until 8.65 s and the chrome draws at 9.15 s. Any key, button or detent cuts it short: the burst into the plate starts at the next
# frame and the chrome draws 0.5 s later. The input that cuts it is acted on as ever: a key sounds (the keys always
# played through the splash), a button or a detent does what it does, once. MIDI and MASTER do not cut it, and a
# touch in the burst or after the splash changes nothing. Each case from a fresh boot (reboot).
#   instrument/build/host/cyma-1_host instrument/tests/splash.txt OUTDIR
#
# ---- left alone: 9.15 s. MASTER and a MIDI note under the credit do not cut it (the note sounds)
wait 100
expect splash 1
expect splash_end 9150
master 2048
midi 90 3C 64
wait 100
expect voices 1
expect midi_notes 1
expect splash_end 9150
midi 80 3C 00
wait 8600
# (8.84 s: the burst) a key in the burst sounds, and the splash ends when it would have
expect splash 1
key 7 down 100
wait 30
expect notes 1
expect splash 1
expect splash_end 9150
key 7 up
wait 400
expect splash 0
expect splash_end 9150
# (after the splash) a button: its page, nothing else
tap ENV
expect page 1
expect splash 0
expect splash_end 9150
#
# ---- a key under the credit (1 s): it sounds, once, and the burst starts at the next frame; the chrome 0.5 s later
reboot
wait 1000
expect splash 1
# (v1.1.1: the boot logo sounds under the credit, its E, its G and the flick's E7 still ringing; it is no player's)
expect logo 2
expect voices 3
key 7 down 100
wait 30
expect notes 1
# (v1.1.1: the key ends the logo too, and sounds behind its 8.7 ms fade: about 15 ms after the key, so the voice
# shows in the UI's copy one frame later than the old 30 ms, below)
expect logo 0
expect splash 1
expect splash_end >1499
expect splash_end <1520
key 7 up
wait 500
expect splash 0
expect splash_end >1499
expect splash_end <1520
expect voices 1
#
# ---- a button in the logo's hold (6 s): the page changes, once (a second ENV would latch layer B), and the cut
reboot
wait 6000
expect splash 1
expect page 0
tap ENV
expect page 1
expect layer 0
expect splash 1
expect splash_end >6499
expect splash_end <6520
wait 500
expect splash 0
expect page 1
expect layer 0
#
# ---- a detent as the logo springs in (5 s): one preset on (not two), and the cut
reboot
wait 5000
expect splash 1
expect preset 1
turn PRESET 1
expect preset 2
expect splash_end >5499
expect splash_end <5520
wait 600
expect loaded 2
expect splash 0
#
# ---- a knob of the page (K1) in the logo's hold (7 s): its value moves, and the cut
reboot
wait 7000
expect splash 1
expect edited 0
spin K1 1
expect edited 1
wait 20
expect splash_end >7499
expect splash_end <7520
wait 500
expect splash 0
#
# ---- reduced motion (GLOBAL MOTION = REDUCE), a key under the credit (0.8 s): the same cut, the credit fading out
# (last: MOTION stays REDUCE in this script's settings)
reboot
param MOTION 1
wait 800
expect splash 1
key 9 down 90
wait 30
expect notes 1
expect splash_end >1299
expect splash_end <1320
key 9 up
wait 500
expect splash 0
expect nan 0
