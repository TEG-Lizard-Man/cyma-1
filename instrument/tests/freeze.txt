# CYMA-1 FREEZE (spec §3.4, §5.3, §8.2): a chord held forever must neither die nor grow. The slots sounding when
# PLAY is pressed take r = 0.9999999; an energy governor holds each under 2x its energy at the freeze (+3 dB).
# Two 20 s freezes, with the room off so the level is the plate's own:
#   freeze_mallet.wav  a struck chord, released once frozen
#   freeze_bow.wav     a bowed chord that keeps being bowed, and leaned on, for 10 s of the freeze: the governor's
#                      real test, since the bow keeps pouring energy in
# tools/golden.py takes 1 s RMS windows between the marks: none more than 3.5 dB over the loudest of the first two
# seconds of the freeze, and the last two seconds no more than 10 dB under them (it held).
#   instrument/build/host/cyma-1_host instrument/tests/freeze.txt OUTDIR
master 4096
wait 1700
tour 0
#
# ---- a struck chord, F3 A3 C4
preset 1
param ROOM 0
wait 400
peakreset
wav24 out:freeze_mallet.wav
wait 200
key 0 down 90
key 4 down 90
key 7 down 90
wait 800
tap PLAY
expect frozen 1
expect ledb_PLAYG 3
key 0 up
key 4 up
key 7 up
#@mark freeze_mallet_start
wait 20000
#@mark freeze_mallet_end
expect frozen 1
expect nan 0
expect peakq <8104001
expect voices >2
# a new note over the drone is an ordinary one: it rings and decays
key 12 down 100
wait 100
key 12 up
wait 1500
tap PLAY
expect frozen 0
expect ledb_PLAYG 0
wait 2000
wav24stop
press REC
wait 600
release REC
#
# ---- a bowed chord, C4 E4 G4, still bowed (and leaned on) through the first half of the freeze
preset 9
param ROOM 0
wait 400
peakreset
wav24 out:freeze_bow.wav
wait 200
key 7 down 90
key 11 down 90
key 14 down 90
wait 300
press_all 60
wait 1700
tap PLAY
expect frozen 1
#@mark freeze_bow_start
press_all 127
wait 4000
press_all 30
wait 3000
press_all 127
wait 3000
key 7 up
key 11 up
key 14 up
wait 10000
#@mark freeze_bow_end
expect frozen 1
expect nan 0
expect peakq <8104001
tap PLAY
expect frozen 0
wait 3000
wav24stop
expect nan 0
metrics
