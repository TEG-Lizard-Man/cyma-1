# CYMA-1 limiter (spec §3.10.5, §3.11, §8.2). The look-ahead limiter reaches the gain a peak needs as the peak
# arrives, so nothing may pass -0.3 dBFS: peakq <= 8,104,000, whatever is thrown at it. Two kinds of render:
#   limiter_drive.wav, limiter_rain.wav   the spec's case: eight fortissimo voices into DRIVE 24 (the bowl struck
#        and leaned on; the thunder sheet rained on as hard as it goes). DRIVE's makeup gain holds a -18 dBFS
#        signal at its level and its curve saturates near 1 x makeup (about -17 dBFS at 24 dB), so these prove
#        the ceiling and the drive, not the limiter's action
#   limiter_hammer.wav, limiter_kick.wav  no drive, LEVEL +6, room off: eight keys struck at 127 six times
#        over (re-strikes add, spec §3.6) and leaned on; then the kick at OCT-2 with its lows tilted up. These
#        are there to drive the limiter: tools/golden.py wants at least one above -1 dBFS (7,471,000) and both
#        under the ceiling. The kick's pitch-drop transients get there (its expect below says so); whether the
#        bowl does depends on the gain staging (spec §3.11: about -18 dBFS a strike), reported on its own
# MASTER is after the limiter (spec §3.11): full here.
#   instrument/build/host/cyma-1_host instrument/tests/limiter.txt OUTDIR
master 4096
wait 1700
tour 0
