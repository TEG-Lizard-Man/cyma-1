# CYMA-1 SURFACE (spec §3.7, §5.4, §5.5, §8.2): SEQ turns the keybed into one plate. Keys strike their own point
# on it (velocity >= 24) or only touch it (below 24: a point damper, no strike); the key LEDs become the live
# vibration map, so the keys on nodal lines go dark. The map is printed ("leds": # bright, + dim or mid, . dark)
# after every gesture; tools/golden.py's "#@leds_any" wants at least one of them with 3 or more keys dark while 3
# or more others are lit (spec §8.2: the nodal map shows, which it does once a few modes dominate, not in the first
# instant of a strike that rings every mode). It also checks that a light touch never sounds a new strike (the
# level just after it is no higher than just before it).
#   instrument/build/host/cyma-1_host instrument/tests/surface.txt OUTDIR
master 4096
wait 1700
tour 0
# from the boot preset: SEQ toggles the key mode (and marks the preset edited)
tap SEQ
expect keymode 1
expect ledb_SEQ 3
expect edited 1
tap SEQ
expect keymode 0
# preset 22 SURFACE GONG starts in SURFACE
preset 22
wait 400
expect keymode 1
expect ledb_SEQ 3
peakreset
wav24 out:surface.wav
wait 200
#@leds_any dark>=3 lit>=3
# ---- strikes: a centre key booms, an edge key shimmers
#@mark surf_centre
key 9 down 100
wait 80
key 9 up
wait 1400
#@mark surf_edge
key 26 down 100
wait 80
key 26 up
wait 1400
# ---- a strike off the centre, then the map once the high modes have died away
#@mark surf_strike
key 4 down 110
wait 80
key 4 up
wait 1500
leds
expect ledb_SEQ 3
# ---- light touches (velocity 10, pressure 30): a damper at that point, never a strike. A touch on a dark key
# keeps only the modes with a node there: the flageolet harmonics
#@mark surf_touch1
key 12 down 10 30
wait 600
leds
key 12 up
wait 300
#@mark surf_touch2
key 16 down 10 30
wait 600
leds
key 16 up
wait 300
#@mark surf_touch3
key 2 down 10 30
wait 600
leds
key 2 up
wait 600
leds
# ---- strike again and lean on the struck key: its pressure damps its own point
key 14 down 100
wait 300
key 14 down 100 80
wait 900
key 14 up
wait 800
leds
wait 1500
leds
# ---- OCT moves the surface root (C3 + 12 x octave): a deeper gong
tap OCT-
expect octave -1
key 9 down 120
wait 80
key 9 up
wait 1500
leds
wait 1500
leds
tap OCT+
expect octave 0
# ---- MIDI notes map to keys (key = note - 53 - 12 x octave); notes off the keybed are ignored
midi 90 3C 64
wait 80
midi 80 3C 00
midi 90 20 64
wait 80
midi 80 20 00
wait 1200
wav24stop
expect nan 0
expect peakq <8104001
expect peakq >265271
# ---- SEQ back to TUNED: the keys play notes again
tap SEQ
expect keymode 0
expect ledb_SEQ 1
key 7 down 100
wait 300
key 7 up
wait 300
expect nan 0
metrics
