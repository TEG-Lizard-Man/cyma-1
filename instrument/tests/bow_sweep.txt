# CYMA-1 bow sweep (spec §3.6, §8.2): the friction junction across its whole range. FORCE {0, 25, 50, 100} %
# x pressure {0, 32, 64, 127} (press_all, the 0..127 of poly aftertouch) on F3, C4, C5 and G5, from preset 09
# BOWED GLASS PAD with the room off (so one cell's tail never props up the next) and DAMPER 100.
# Each cell: the key lands at velocity 100, the lean comes 100 ms later and holds 1.9 s; then REC (DAMP) clears
# the plate for 0.5 s. tools/golden.py checks every cell: no NaN, RMS <= -3 dBFS while bowed, and RMS >= -30 dBFS
# from 1 s on wherever FORCE >= 50 and pressure >= 64. G5 (key 26) is on the top row, which senses velocity only:
# its bow pressure is velocity x swell whatever the lean (spec §3.6 P_eff), so its rows repeat by design.
#   instrument/build/host/cyma-1_host instrument/tests/bow_sweep.txt OUTDIR
master 4096
wait 1700
tour 0
preset 9
wait 400
param ROOM 0
param DAMPER 100
wait 400
expect exciter 1
