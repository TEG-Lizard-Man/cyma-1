## How these are made

- `tools/golden.py` runs every scenario through `build/host/cyma-1_host`, then measures the renders with numpy
  and scipy: FFT peak ratios against Bessel and Euler, a Welch spectrum above 0.45 fs, sample peaks, a
  high-passed click detector, a Schroeder-curve T60 of `src/fx.c` driven by an impulse, and `demo_hash` on two
  host runs and in wasm (`tools/wasm_smoke.mjs`).
- `tools/spec.py` writes `build/spec/SPEC.json` (the page's spec card) and this file.
- `tools/run_all.sh` does all of it, from a clean build. With `--device` it also builds the package and writes
  `build/SHA256SUMS`; with `--perf`, it times that package in fm1-emu (`tools/emu_perf.py`).
