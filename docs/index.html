    How to play

    Every key strikes, bows or rains on a virtual metal plate. The sand on the screen settles into the nodal figure you are hearing, because the picture and the sound are the same modal simulation.

    Keys

    WC♯ ED♯ TF♯ YG♯ UA♯ OC♯ PD♯ AC4 SD DE FF GG HA JB KC5 LD ;E 'F

    A plays C4 and the row continues to the apostrophe, F5, as in Ableton Live; W, E, T, Y, U, O and P are the sharps.

    • Space holdLean in: every held key pressed harder
    • Z XOctave down, up
    • C VVelocity −20, +20
    • ShiftSEL (layer B and the combos)
    • ← →SELECT: the shape
    • [ ]ALGORITHM: the exciter

    • 7–0 -SEQ ARP PLAY REC SAVE
    • BBig screen
    • EscLet go of everything: latches off, all notes off

    Mouse, touch and pen

    • Click a key. Higher on the key is softer, lower is harder. In SURFACE mode the very top edge only touches.
    • Drag down on a held key to lean in. A pen leans by its pressure, and so does a Force Touch trackpad in Safari.
    • Shift-click or right-click a key or button to latch it down (Alt-click too); click again to let go.
    • Knobs: drag up and down, or scroll over them. MASTER is the volume.
    • The screen: click it for the big screen.
    • MIDI: play it from a controller (MIDI In), or send its notes and pressure to your DAW (MIDI Out).

    The panel

    • SELECTShape: drum, circle, hexagon, square, triangle, star, guitar, stadium, bar
    • ALGORITHMExciter: mallet, bow, rain
    • PRESETS24 objects, then My Sounds (U01–U32)
    • K1–K4The page's four parameters; hold SEL for four more
    • HOME ENV FX LFO GLOPages: plate, excite, space, motion, global
    • SEQSURFACE: the keybed becomes the plate
    • PLAYFreeze the plate ringing
    • EDITView: sand, field, mesh
    • REC · ARPDamp while held · latch
    • SELShift
